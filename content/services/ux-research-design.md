## What you get

Design decisions backed by **evidence, not taste alone**. Customer interviews and journey
mapping reveal what your visitors actually need; wireframes and UI design turn that into
pages where the next click is always obvious.

## Why research before design?

Because redesigns based on guesses get re-redesigned. A few hours of structured research —
who the customer is, what they fear, what convinces them — saves weeks of decoration that
doesn't convert.

## How does UX connect to email?

The same person who lands on your website later opens your emails. Consistent journeys —
the promise on the landing page matching the story in the welcome flow — are what turn
one-time visitors into repeat buyers.
