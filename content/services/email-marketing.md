## What you get

An email system that works while you sleep. I set up the flows every store needs
(**welcome series**, **abandoned cart**, regular **newsletters**), organize your list
into **segments** that actually mean something, and run **A/B tests** so each month
performs better than the last.

## Which flows should a small store start with?

1. **Welcome flow** — 3–5 emails that introduce the brand and guide the first purchase.
2. **Abandoned cart flow** — recovers shoppers who left checkout without buying.
3. **Newsletter rhythm** — consistent campaigns that keep the list warm and buying.

## Klaviyo or Mailchimp?

Both, depending on your store. Klaviyo is the stronger fit for data-driven e-commerce;
Mailchimp works well for simpler lists and smaller budgets. I'll recommend honestly
after seeing your setup — see my article *[Klaviyo vs Mailchimp](/blog/klaviyo-vs-mailchimp/)*.
