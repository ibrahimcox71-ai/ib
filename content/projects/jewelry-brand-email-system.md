## The challenge

A jewelry brand had an email list but no system: occasional newsletters, no flows, and one
undifferentiated list receiving every message. Interest faded between campaigns and revenue
from email was left on the table.

## What I delivered

- **Welcome flow** — introduced the brand story and guided new subscribers toward a first purchase.
- **Abandoned cart flow** — follow-ups for shoppers who left checkout, timed and worded for a considered purchase like jewelry.
- **Newsletter cadence** — consistent campaigns with a clear job each: launch, educate, or convert.

## How performance was improved

- **Smarter segmentation** — the list was restructured into meaningful segments (by engagement
  and purchase behavior), so each group received emails relevant to where they were in the journey.
- **Structured A/B testing** — campaigns were tested one variable at a time (subject lines, content
  blocks, send approach) so wins stacked up instead of canceling out.

**Result:** campaign performance improved as emails became more relevant to each segment.

> Honest note: every list is different. What transferred here is the *process* — research,
> segment, test one variable, let the data decide — not any single tactic.
